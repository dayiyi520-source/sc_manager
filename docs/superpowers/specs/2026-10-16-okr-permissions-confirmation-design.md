# OKR roles and confirmation

Administrator and sub-administrator roles receive individually configurable grants for OKR templates, review templates, roles, weekly cycles, confirmation settings, and company goal editing. Existing administrators migrate to all grants; old company editors migrate to company editing. Company editing adds capability without removing personal goals. Supervisors retain department and personal goals.

Confirmation requests and lists use the current effective reviewer. An old open request remains blocked on submit if assignment changes. Administrators remain exempt. Owner, effective reviewer and administrator can see hidden pending requests. Role settings allow checkbox grant editing, loading, error and retry. Validate migrations, revocations, reviewer changes, typecheck, build, and PRD.
